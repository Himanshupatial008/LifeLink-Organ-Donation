01 / UNDERSTANDING ORGAN DONATION

What does it
really mean?

Organ donation is the process of giving an organ or tissue to help someone whose own organ is failing.

Donation may take place after death, or in some cases while a person is living. Doctors assess medical suitability and follow strict legal and ethical procedures. The decision to donate is personal, and learning the facts helps you make an informed choice.

02 / THE GIFT OF GIVING

What can be donated?

Depending on medical circumstances, different organs and tissues may help people in need.

♡

Heart

Can help a person with severe heart disease.

◉

Lungs

May help people with serious lung conditions.

◒

Liver

A portion may be donated by a living donor in selected cases.

◐

Kidneys

A living donor may be able to donate one kidney.

✧

Corneas

Can restore sight for people with certain corneal conditions.

⌁

Other tissues

Skin, bone, heart valves and other tissues may also be donated.

The organs and tissues that can be donated depend on medical assessment, applicable law and the circumstances of donation.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Can anyone become an organ donor?

People should express their wishes regardless of age or medical history. Medical professionals determine suitability at the appropriate time.

Does registering guarantee donation?

No. Registration records an intention. Actual donation depends on medical suitability, consent requirements and applicable law.

Can living people donate?

In selected circumstances, living donation may be possible, such as donating one kidney or a part of the liver. It requires careful medical and legal assessment.

Will doctors stop treatment if I am a donor?

Medical teams focus on saving the patient's life. Donation is considered only under defined medical and legal conditions.

Where can I register officially in India?

Visit the National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organization (NOTTO) website at notto.mohfw.gov.in and follow the official guidance available there.
